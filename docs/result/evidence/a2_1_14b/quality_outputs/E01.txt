Designing a multi-region High Availability (HA) gateway architecture with zero downtime involves several key components and strategies to ensure that the system remains operational and responsive even in the event of failures. Below is a high-level overview of how you might approach this:

### 1. **Architecture Overview**

- **Regions**: Deploy the gateway in multiple geographically distributed regions to ensure low latency and high availability.
- **Load Balancers**: Use load balancers to distribute incoming traffic across multiple instances of the gateway.
- **Auto Scaling**: Implement auto-scaling to handle varying loads and ensure that the system can scale up or down based on demand.
